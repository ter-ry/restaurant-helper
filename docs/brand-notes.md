# Flowtally Brand Notes

## Brand Name

Flowtally

## Meaning

Flowtally combines two ideas:

- Flow: the movement of restaurant records across POS reports, invoices, delivery apps, paper notes, spreadsheets, and accountant requests.
- Tally: the practical act of counting, checking, and organizing records so owners can understand where money is going.

The name should feel simple, useful, and approachable for busy restaurant owners.

## Positioning Statement

Flowtally is a restaurant admin and money-visibility service for independent restaurants, cafes, bakeries, takeout shops, bubble tea shops, food trucks, and small restaurant groups.

Flowtally helps owners save time on admin and get a clearer view of where money is going by organizing records around invoices, daily close, delivery payouts, expenses, reports, and bookkeeping handoff.

## Tagline Options

- Save time on admin. See where money is going.
- Restaurant admin, made easier to follow.
- Clearer records for busy food businesses.
- Less admin after service. Better visibility before month-end.
- Keep restaurant records moving clearly.
- Admin flow. Money clarity.

Recommended tagline:

```text
Save time on admin. See where money is going.
```

## Voice And Tone

- Plainspoken
- Local
- Useful
- Calm
- Practical
- Honest about being early
- Respectful of how busy restaurant owners are

Use concrete restaurant language:

- invoices
- daily close
- delivery payouts
- paper notes
- spreadsheets
- accountant requests
- bookkeeper handoff
- spending visibility

## Brand Personality

Flowtally should feel like a practical operator who understands restaurants, not a flashy tech product.

It should be:

- trustworthy
- approachable
- organized
- grounded
- service-minded
- professional enough for Instagram outreach

## Words To Avoid

- AI-powered
- revolutionary
- disrupt
- autonomous
- enterprise
- platform
- optimize everything
- financial intelligence
- machine learning
- hyper-growth
- seamless
- all-in-one
- replace your POS
- replace your accountant

## Future Brand Direction

Flowtally can grow from a service-led pilot into a lightweight product around restaurant admin records and money visibility.

Future directions:

- visual record dashboard
- delivery payout checks
- invoice and spending summaries
- daily close review
- bookkeeper-ready handoff
- simple weekly owner reports

Keep the brand anchored in restaurant owner outcomes, not technology claims.
