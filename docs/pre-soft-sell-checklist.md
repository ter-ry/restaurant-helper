# Flowtally Pre-Soft-Sell Checklist

Use this checklist before sharing the public landing page or demo with growing independent restaurants.

## ICP and Messaging

- Landing page speaks to growing independent restaurants, not every small food business.
- Copy mentions staff/team workflows, recurring invoices, multiple suppliers, inventory, supplier prices, and reordering.
- POS and accounting are framed as staying in place without sounding defensive.
- No academic wording such as "operational gap" or "operational layer."

## Demo Continuity

- Dashboard tells the one-week story first: spend, spend trend, price changes, reorder needs, close status.
- Purchases shows invoice/receipt upload, OCR review, item confirmation, saved history, and inventory handoff.
- Inventory shows stock changes, receiving, counts, waste/adjustments, and reorder needs.
- Menu & Costing separates true margin risk from missing setup.
- Close & Reports clearly shows daily close and CSV readiness.

## Demo Realism

- Current-week and prior-week purchasing comparisons are believable.
- No impossible values such as `-100%` spend change unless intentionally explained.
- No unrealistic menu margins such as `-190%`.
- Supplier names, categories, inventory items, and reorder needs feel like one restaurant.

## Mobile and UX

- Landing page CTAs are easy to tap.
- Demo hamburger drawer opens and closes cleanly.
- Dashboard workflow reads vertically on narrow screens.
- Modals and drawers are usable on a phone width.
- No horizontal overflow on core demo pages.

## Claims and Scope

- No live POS integration is implied.
- No live QuickBooks/accounting sync is implied.
- No supplier ordering/sending is implied.
- Future functionality is labeled as planned, preview, future integration, or draft only.
