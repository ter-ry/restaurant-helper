# First private-alpha pilot onboarding

## Restaurant supplies

- organization name, location name, timezone, and operating contacts
- Google account access for each owner/manager
- Square account and permission to connect the production Square application
- inventory list, suppliers, units, starting quantities, and initial stock count
- recipes/menu items and selling prices
- first invoices and any known waste/adjustments

## Flowtally/setup operator performs

- create the production organization and location
- verify timezone and role memberships
- confirm Google and Square callbacks
- connect Square and map locations/catalog items
- load inventory and suppliers
- build recipes and map menu items
- receive first invoices
- perform the opening stock count
- verify Daily Close and reporting permissions

## Can wait

- historical invoice backfill beyond the agreed opening period
- optional reports/export refinements
- additional locations and advanced menu cleanup

Before handoff, complete a login/logout test, a purchase read/receive test, an inventory read, a Square mapping read, a stock-count read, and a Daily Close read. Do not share staging credentials with the restaurant.
