# Flowtally Production Showcase

## The idea

Flowtally is the operational control layer between a restaurant’s POS, supplier invoices, inventory, daily close, and accounting/export workflow. It helps an owner see what changed and what needs action.

## Three connected moments

1. Supplier invoice/OCR creates reviewable purchasing evidence and an inventory movement.
2. A mapped Square variation connects a completed sale to recipe ingredients and usage.
3. Low stock, price changes, and review states become an owner attention loop: count, reorder, review, or follow up.

## Why it matters

Small restaurants often have the data in several places but not the handoff between them. Flowtally makes the handoff visible without asking the operator to replace the POS or become an accountant.

## What this showcase proves

The walkthrough proves the product shape and the control points. It should claim live integration only where the current production UI visibly verifies it. Reporting and any blank or unverified workflow are out of scope for this presentation.

## The ask

Identify the first control worth piloting: purchasing variance, inventory/reorder control, Square-to-recipe usage, or daily close follow-up.
